Field Note №01 / The Method

The brain doesn't
keep time.
It entrains to it.

Every consistent golf swing shares one signature: a backswing that takes three times as long as the downswing. Twenty-four frames up. Eight frames down. A 3-to-1 ratio that the world's best players never have to think about — because their nervous system already knows it.

Music written at this exact tempo doesn't entertain. It entrains. It synchronizes the basal ganglia, dampens the prefrontal chatter that ruins a routine, and primes the dopamine loop to fire on follow-through rather than fault-finding.

We didn't invent the rhythm. We just made it listenable.
