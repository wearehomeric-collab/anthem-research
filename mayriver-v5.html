248 · Anthem System · v1.0

A pacing score, engineered for the round.

The 24/8 framework is a neuro-acoustic system tuned to the mechanics of a golf swing — twenty-four frames of backswing, eight frames of downswing, a 3:1 tempo ratio that runs at 112 BPM. Its purpose is to entrain the basal ganglia rhythm circuits that govern motor sequencing, so the swing becomes a beat the body already knows.

The track is structured around an anticipation–release musical arc that mirrors the swing's preparation–strike phase. The brain's dopamine response to musical resolution coincides with impact — reward arrives at the moment the ball does.
