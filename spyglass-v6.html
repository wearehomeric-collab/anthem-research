06|Strategy

Two courses, one routing.

Spyglass operates on two distinct engines. The first five holes, routed through the dunes of Stevenson's inspiration, favor a links-hybrid mindset: flight down, carry controlled, commit to the line even when the wind argues.

From the 6th onward, the routing turns inland into Del Monte Forest and becomes a different test. Narrow corridors between Monterey pines reward the player who can shape the ball both ways off the tee.

Green complexes are Poa annua — bumpy by afternoon, grain-heavy near the coast. The winning score correlates most strongly with strokes gained: approach from 150–200 yards, the range that sits near every defensive pin on the back nine.

The tempo demand is real. Slow play, cold mornings, and cypress shadows conspire against rhythm. This course rewards players who find a tempo and protect it.
